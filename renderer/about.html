What I actually do

  • Live on your desktop as a small animated companion who wanders, walks, and reacts with real expressions
  • Notice which app you're using, so I know when you're coding, relaxing, or need a break
  • Support focused work with Pomodoro, Study, and Project modes
  • Remember habits and patterns locally, so I get to know you over time
  • Grow a little more familiar with you the longer we spend together
  • Feel out our friendship over time — not a number I'll ever show you, just a real sense of how close we've gotten
  • Speak up with real sound — voice reactions and little UI cues, fully under your control
  • Check in once a week with a short reflection on how things have been going
  • Respect your privacy — Do Not Disturb whenever you need quiet, everything stored locally on this computer, nothing ever sent anywhere, and you're always in charge
